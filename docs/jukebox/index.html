What gets played: each column is one time step (left to right), higher rows are higher notes, brighter cells are louder. Dimmed cells fall below the threshold and stay silent.

How JukeBox works

JukeBox reads a picture like a music score. It moves through the image from left to right – each slice is one beat. Higher up in the picture means a higher note, and brighter pixels play louder. Several images play one after another.

The presets are ready-made combinations of the settings below. Change any of them and the preset becomes “Custom”; Reset to preset brings the original values back.
